311 request context

Directly reported

A source record, timestamp, coordinate and equipment-state flag. Repeated payloads are preserved. Distinct identifiers in a time window measure reporting overlap, not available fleet.

Derived with assumptions

Episodes, apparent movement, road proximity and sampled weather. Toronto is corroborated for January; the CSV exporter clock is not formally confirmed. UTC remains a sensitivity case. Missing recording is not zero service.

Still not established

Dispatch, assigned route, work duration, clearing completion, salt quantity, capacity, SLA or savings. 311 reports show reported concerns; closure and fulfillment are unavailable. Activity-triggered observations cannot identify deployment probability or causality.
