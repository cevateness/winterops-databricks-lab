04 / Historical weather context

ERA5-requested reanalysis at returned grids. Means/ranges use distinct grid/time facts, without observation-count weighting.

Blue: selected interpretation; orange: alternative clock interpretation. Gaps stay gaps. Every plotted value is a mean of the sampled grids for that recorded source hour. The day selector above also filters this plot.

Table shows the first 12 recorded hours for the selected weeks; select one day above to inspect all of its recorded hours. Export includes every filtered hour.

Weather was collected where and when activity was recorded. This selection bias prevents treating the sample as continuous citywide weather. Precipitation is not summed across locations, and overlapping two-hour antecedent windows are not added together. Reanalysis is retrospective and must not be used as decision-time forecast features.

05 / Evidence & assumptions

Verified source and join semantics travel with the snapshot.

Clock evidence

The source TIMESTAMP_NTZ fields remain unchanged. January samples do not establish DST-edge behavior or the original exporter's complete clock contract.

Weather provenance

Grid/elevation selection is coarse; small weather differences do not imply street-level information. Provider/model identity is attested by the request parameter.

Interpretation limits

Counts are not labor hours, road coverage, productivity, capacity, workload demand or causal effects. Missing telemetry is not inactivity. Contracted equipment may be absent from the source.

Four weeks are a descriptive learning sample, not a sufficient predictive evaluation set.

Source versions, processing keys and reproducibility

Native four-week Gold publication remains blocked by inaccessible private approval matrices and secure workspace bindings. No views, aliases, Jobs, ingestion tables or permissions were changed by this product.
