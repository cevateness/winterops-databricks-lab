Reporting, episodes & weather.

Four historical weeks · descriptive diagnostics V2 · real pinned data

Distinct reporting identifiers within a window are not instantaneous concurrency or fleet availability. Observed episode spans are not work duration. Missing telemetry is never zero activity. Weather is activity-conditioned reanalysis; Toronto remains a corroborated clock hypothesis.

Day-level reporting intensity

Bars: mean distinct reporting IDs per occupied window within a day, not summed fleet counts. Bucket quantiles use linear interpolation; missing windows are excluded, not imputed zero.

2 / Heuristic observed episodes

Gap sensitivity

Observed onset by local hour of day

Each truck/source is segmented independently. First and last observations censor the actual onset/completion. Tied timestamps preserve observation multiplicity; contradictory states are not resolved by arbitrary record order. Three thresholds are alternatives, not additive episodes.

3 / Consecutive observed equipment states

Matrix includes same-state persistence. Only known consecutive timestamp groups within the limit contribute. Source starts, longer gaps and conflicting ties are counted separately as unknown; no activation is inferred across them.

4 / Sampled weather relationships

Unit: activity cell/hour. Distinct grid windows may share weather facts across adjacent intervals and are serially dependent. Shares use observation counts; mean cell-hour counts give each recorded cell/hour one unit. No formal significance or causal estimate is claimed.

5 / Geographic playback and disclosure boundary

The public coarse week map keeps its existing fixed grain. Day/hour/equipment playback and returned weather grid overlays are generated only in private_map.html from the private build. No detailed spatial records, identifiers, episode timelines or private map exports are embedded here.

Private playback uses actual coarse geographic cell bounds and returned grid centres in an offline Web Mercator diagram. It needs no basemap or external tiles. Existing public aggregates cannot be made secret retroactively; V2 adds no new spatial filter intersections or exports.
